DATA DOWNLOAD GUIDE
How to get your LibreView File
Download the historical glucose data from your LibreView account.
  1. Open LibreView.com and sign in to the account connected to your Libre app or reader.
  2. Open Glucose History.
  3. Choose Download Glucose Data and follow any verification prompts.
  4. Choose Download to save the data file to your computer.
  5. Go back to GLUCOLAB and click Choose file under LibreView glucose data; select the downloaded File.
Use the raw glucose export, not a screenshot or PDF report. Never share sensitive glucose exports publicly.
Reference: Abbott FreeStyle: downloading glucose data from LibreView. These instructions describe the providers' export features; interfaces may differ by device and region.

DATA DOWNLOAD GUIDE
How to get your Medtronic File
Export your insulin-pump records from Medtronic CareLink Personal.
  1. Sign in to your CareLink Personal account and make sure your pump data is uploaded.
  2. Open Reports and choose the period containing the data you want.
  3. Find Data Export (CSV) (often shown near the bottom right of the Reports screen).
  4. Download the exported file and save it on your computer.
  5. Return to GLUCOLAB and click Choose file under Medtronic pump data; select the exported File.
The exported pump data describes recorded device events; it does not necessarily contain your verified programmed basal profile. Keep the file private.
Reference: Medtronic CareLink Personal guide: Data Export (CSV). These instructions describe the providers' export features; interfaces may differ by device and region.
